Pillar of Salah
بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ
Bismi-llāhi r-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm
"In the Name of Allah, the Entirely Merciful, the Especially Merciful."
Roots: Ism (Name) • R-Ḥ-M (Mercy, Compassion)
الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ الْعَالَمِينَ
Al-ḥamdu li-llāhi Rabbi l-'ālamīn
"All praise and gratitude is due to Allah, Lord of all creation."
Roots: Ḥ-M-D (Praise/Thanks) • R-B-B (Lord, Sustainer) • '-L-M (The World/Creation)
الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ
Ar-Raḥmāni r-Raḥīm
"The Entirely Merciful, the Especially Merciful."
Roots: Raḥmān (Universal grace) • Raḥīm (Enduring specific mercy)
مَالِكِ يَوْمِ الدِّينِ
Māliki yawmi d-dīn
"Master and Sovereign of the Day of Judgment."
Roots: M-L-K (Sovereignty/Ownership) • Y-W-M (Day) • D-Y-N (Judgment/Account)
إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ وَإِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِينُ
Iyyāka na'budu wa iyyāka nasta'īn
"You alone we worship, and You alone we ask for help."
Roots: '-B-D (Worship/Devotion) • '-W-N (Help/Assistance)
اهْدِنَا الصِّرَاطَ الْمُسْتَقِيمَ
Ihdinā ṣ-ṣirāṭa l-mustaqīm
"Guide us to the straight and upright path."
Roots: H-D-Y (Guidance) • Ṣ-R-Ṭ (Path) • Q-W-M (Straight/Upright)
صِرَاطَ الَّذِينَ أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ غَيْرِ الْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ وَلَا الضَّالِّينَ
Ṣirāṭa lladhīna an'amta 'alayhim, ghayri l-maghḍūbi 'alayhim wa la ḍ-ḍāllīn
"The path of those upon whom You bestowed favor, not of those who earned anger, nor of those who are astray."
Roots: N-'-M (Blessing/Favor) • Gh-Ḍ-B (Anger) • Ḍ-L-L (Astray)
